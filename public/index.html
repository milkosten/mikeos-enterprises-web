Straight from the source. Open by law.

🇸🇪 Sweden — Bolagsverket & SCB

The complete Swedish company registry: 904,910 companies and 107,899 storefront establishments, including each firm’s registered business description (verksamhetsbeskrivning) and SNI industry codes.

Loaded from the official bulk registry files and refreshed periodically. Geocoding of establishments is in progress — more pins appear on the map every day.

Source: Bolagsverket · SCB — Swedish open government data, free to use and share.
Company profiles are enriched live from SCB’s Allmänna företagsregistret (Statistics Sweden).

🇫🇷 France — SIRENE (INSEE)

France’s national registry, map-first: 1.65 million geocoded storefronts — every boulangerie, librairie and café with a front door, from the official SIRENE stock and geolocation files. Refreshed from INSEE’s monthly bulk releases.

Name search runs on the same registry; area browse shows what’s actually on the street, city by city.

Source: INSEE — SIRENE — Licence Ouverte / Open Licence 2.0, free to use and share with attribution.
